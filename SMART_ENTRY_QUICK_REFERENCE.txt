10 PRODUCTION-READY INTELLIGENCE FEATURES
================================================================================

1. SmartEntryFilter
   Purpose: Multi-condition entry validation
   Checks: RSI < 70, price < 3% above EMA20, volume > MA20, daily uptrend, MTF agreement
   Usage: should_enter, reason = filter.should_enter(symbol, market_type, candles_1h, 4h, 1d)

2. ATRPositionSizer
   Purpose: Volatility-adjusted position sizing
   Logic: Risk % / ATR%; reduce by 50% if ATR% > 5%
   Usage: size = sizer.calculate_position_size(candles, balance, risk_pct=0.02)

3. MarketRegimeFilter
   Purpose: Overall market condition detection
   Returns: "bull" (price > EMA200), "bear" (price < EMA200), or "range"
   Usage: regime = filter.get_regime(btc_daily_candles)

4. FibonacciDCA
   Purpose: Safety order spacing using Fibonacci ratios
   Levels: 1%, 2.3%, 3.8%, 6.2%, 10% below entry
   Usage: levels = dca.get_safety_order_levels(entry_price, num_orders=5)

5. TrailingTakeProfit
   Purpose: Adaptive TP management during price moves
   Phases: <2%→hold | 2-4%→breakeven | ≥4%→trail 1.5%
   Usage: action, new_tp = ttp.update(current_price, entry_price, current_tp)

6. CooldownManager
   Purpose: Prevents rapid re-entry after losses
   Default: 30-minute cooldown
   Usage: cooldown.record_loss(symbol); if cooldown.can_trade(symbol): ...

7. DailyDealLimiter
   Purpose: Limit deals per bot per day
   Default: Max 5 deals/day, resets at midnight
   Usage: if limiter.can_open_deal(bot_id): limiter.record_deal(bot_id)

8. KellyCriterion
   Purpose: Optimal position sizing formula
   Formula: f* = (bp - q) / b; uses 25% Kelly (conservative)
   Usage: fraction = kelly.optimal_fraction(win_rate, avg_win, avg_loss)

9. DrawdownCircuitBreaker
   Purpose: Stop all trading if drawdown exceeds limit
   Default: 10% max drawdown
   Usage: if breaker.check(portfolio_value, peak_value): continue_trading()

10. SharpeCalculator
    Purpose: Risk-adjusted return metric
    Formula: (avg_return - rfr) / std_dev(returns)
    Usage: sharpe = calc.calculate(daily_returns, risk_free_rate=0.05)

================================================================================
CUSTOMIZATION
================================================================================

All parameters are configurable:

SmartEntryFilter:
  - rsi_overbought_level=70
  - price_distance_pct=0.03
  - volume_ma_period=20
  - trend_ema_period=200

ATRPositionSizer:
  - atr_period=14
  - volatility_threshold_pct=0.05
  - volatility_reduce_factor=0.5

MarketRegimeFilter:
  - ema_period=200

CooldownManager:
  - cooldown_minutes=30

DailyDealLimiter:
  - max_deals_per_day=5

KellyCriterion:
  - kelly_fraction=0.25  (uses 25% of full Kelly)

DrawdownCircuitBreaker:
  - max_drawdown_pct=0.10

SharpeCalculator:
  - risk_free_rate=0.05

Example:
  filter = SmartEntryFilter(rsi_overbought_level=75, price_distance_pct=0.05)
